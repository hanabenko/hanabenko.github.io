Hi, I’m <span style="color:#6c3cff; font-weight:600;">Hana Benko</span> - a computer science student at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/) interested in building technology that improves access to learning. I’ve worked in human–computer interaction research through the [HCII](https://www.hcii.cmu.edu/), developed educational tools for nonprofits, taught Python as a [Stanford Code in Place](https://codeinplace.stanford.edu/) section leader, and helped run technical programs with [ScottyLabs](https://scottylabs.org/). I care about designing intuitive learning experiences, exploring how people interact with technology, and creating tools that make complex ideas feel approachable. This blog is where I share thoughts, projects, and things I’m curious about.
